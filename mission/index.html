The category: Decision Intelligence
for the Experience Economy

Why this category is real

Gartner published its first-ever Magic Quadrant for Decision Intelligence Platforms in January 2026. Their definition — "combines data, analytics and AI to create decision flows that support and automate complex judgments" — is literally our loop.

Why the space is ours

Every DI leader is horizontal enterprise — Aera does supply chains, Quantexa does financial crime. Nobody has built DI for the experience economy. That empty vertical quadrant is the company.

✓ Statement v1.1 · decided 2026-08-18

The statement — locked (one word under test)

Flowz is for event and festival producers* who see their truth scattered across ten tools, so problems surface late, insights don't become action, and the team never moves as one. Flowz is a vertical decision-intelligence platform that turns ticketing, marketing, community and finance data into ready-to-run actions: insights that become flows, tasks and campaigns, fast enough to matter. Unlike generic CRMs, BI dashboards and spreadsheets, which store data and wait for you to figure it out, our product acts: it reads the signal and runs the flow, preloaded with event-sector playbooks and market behavior data no horizontal tool has.

✓ Need slot decided — cause → three effects 🧪 *Under test: the WHO word

The WHO test Elad added (2026-08-18): does our customer actually call themselves a producer? Alternatives to test in the interviews: "events & festivals teams" · "entertainment & experiences teams/organizers". We listen for the word they use about themselves — the statement adopts their word, not ours. The choice also decides who we're speaking to: the owner alone, or the whole team (buyer ≠ user).

The 10-user protocol (inside the interviews we already planned)

1. Problem-first, Mom-Test style — never open with the sentence.  2. Show the sentence + WHY only at the end: "what would you expect this to do for you?"  3. Pass = they name a real, recent situation where they'd want it. Fail = polite nodding, "sounds cool."  4. The label test (added 2026-08-18): listen for the word they use about themselves — producer? events & festivals team? experiences organizer? Their word takes the WHO slot.  5.
