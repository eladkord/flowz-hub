The category: Decision Intelligence
for the Experience Economy

Why this category is real

Gartner published its first-ever Magic Quadrant for Decision Intelligence Platforms in January 2026. Their definition — "combines data, analytics and AI to create decision flows that support and automate complex judgments" — is literally our loop.

Why the space is ours

Every DI leader is horizontal enterprise — Aera does supply chains, Quantexa does financial crime. Nobody has built DI for the experience economy. That empty vertical quadrant is the company.

Data→Insights→Actions→Flows

Where we stand — written 2026-07-20

The statement we already have

Flowz is for event and festival producers in the experience economy who are losing buyers they already won — and can't see why, because their truth is scattered across ten tools and every decision lands on them alone. Flowz is a vertical decision-intelligence platform that turns ticketing, marketing, community and finance data into ready-to-run actions — insights that become flows, tasks and campaigns, fast enough to matter. Unlike generic CRMs, BI dashboards and spreadsheets — which store data and wait for you to figure it out, our product acts: it reads the signal and runs the flow, pre-loaded with event-sector playbooks and market behavior data no horizontal tool has.
