LATER · Phase 3 — Agentic (M4–M7)

Insights & Flows grow a brain — in two deliberate steps

Step 1 · Learn the market (M4–M5)

Deep analysis of toMix's cross-segment data — events, tourism, culinary, family, festivals — concluding in two proprietary assets:

  • Market Playbook — what works, when, for which audience, at what price curve
  • Behavioral Patterns library — recurring buyer behaviors: timing, triggers, churn signatures, upgrade paths

Step 2 · Feed the engine (M5–M7)

Insights & Flows upgrade from algorithmic to agentic: AI reasoning grounded in the 100-item bank + the Playbook + the Patterns library. The system starts proposing flows it composed itself — always through approval gates, widening autonomy only as trust is earned.

Sequencing is principled: agentic only after algorithmic proves out — concrete and measurable before clever.

Internal
· The Playbook + Patterns derived from toMix data are exactly the "derived insights" of data-license clause A.7 — perpetual, anonymized, usable by Global worldwide. This phase is where the deal's data value materializes; guard the clause.
· Agentic Lead hire ideally lands M3–M4 so ramp-up meets Step 1.
· This slide is also the answer to "what makes you Palantir-for-experiences and not another CRM" — vision ammunition for any investor conversation.

Dependencies & Risks — the honesty slide

What this timeline assumes, and what can move it

Hard dependencies

  • Incorporation → platform approvals. Business Verification needs a registered entity; it gates production-grade Meta/WhatsApp access. Partner-carried pilots bridge the gap.
  • DPA before the first data file. Non-negotiable, and quick if prioritized.
  • Client-side authorizations. Ad accounts, WhatsApp numbers, ticketing API access — toMix actions on the critical path.
  • Integration validation before deployment — step 3 of the assembly line, always.

Working assumptions

  • Parallel execution across workstreams — the two hires make this real
  • Historical data quality is workable (identity resolution absorbs mess, not chaos)
  • Ticketing write-back lands where the platform allows — scope confirmed in Phase 0
  • Sequence holds even if a phase stretches: nothing downstream builds on sand
Internal
· This slide doubles as our protection: every dependency named here that sits on toMix's side (authorizations, data quality, ticketing access) is a documented reason a milestone slips without it being our miss — which matters because earn-in tranches and budget releases ride these milestones.
· Incorporation timing is now product-critical — another reason the founders' agreement + formation move this month.
